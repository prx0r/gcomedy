## The book in one paragraph

Attardo organizes humor studies into three layers (retrieval, comprehension, appreciation), reviews three classical theories (release, incongruity, aggression), then builds the GTVH (General Theory of Verbal Humor) as the linguistic formalization. Part II covers competence (semantics, GTVH, pragmatics, verbal humor). Part III covers performance (delivery, conversation, sociolinguistics). Part IV covers applications (literature, translation, classroom).

## Key concepts → our vocabulary

| Attardo concept | Definition | Our equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Script Opposition (SO)** | Two semantic scripts that overlap and oppose | `double_interpret` operator, GTVH SO |
| **Logical Mechanism (LM)** | The reasoning that connects SO to the punch | GTVH LM (12 options: garden_path, juxtaposition...) |
| **Situation (SI)** | The contextual setting of the joke | premise, setup world |
| **Target (TA)** | Who/what the joke is about | jokegate TARGETS (self/powerful/species/victim) |
| **Narrative Strategy (NS)** | How the joke is told (rhythm, structure) | timing doctrine, delivery shape |
| **Language (LA)** | The specific words used | word choice, syllable count |
| **Incongruity** | Divergence from expectations | punch surprise, semantic_distance |
| **Resolution** | The re-interpretation that makes the incongruity funny | punch reframe, LM firing |
| **Bisociation** (Koestler) | Simultaneous perception in two incompatible frames | double_interpret, BV simultaneous |
| **Appropriate incongruity** (Oring) | Incongruity that is both surprising AND fitting | BV sweet spot |
| **Retrieval (level 1)** | Accessing the joke from memory | (not our focus) |
| **Comprehension (level 2)** | Understanding the joke's structure | agent gates (slop/edgy/feel) |
| **Appreciation (level 3)** | Finding the joke funny | audience response, laugh |

## The three classical theories (Ch 3, mapped to us)

| Theory | Core claim | Our mapping | Testable as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Release** (Freud, Spencer) | Laughter discharges nervous energy | pause-before-punch (tension buildup → release) | speech_rate decrease + pause increase before punchline |
| **Incongruity** (Kant, Schopenhauer, Morreall) | Humor from expectation violation | punch surprise, garden_path, semantic_distance | punchline_surprisal, setup_uncertainty |
| **Aggression** (Hobbes, Plato, Gruner) | Laughter at others' misfortune | status_invert, target selection | status_delta, target_is_performer |

**Attardo's position:** these are complementary, not competing. They describe different aspects of the same phenomenon. (Maps to our multi-layer BV model.)
