# WHAT NIBBĀNA IS FOR ÑĀṆANANDA — the vortex register
*Ñāṇananda's nibbāna, stated in the modern dynamical-systems register (the vortex /
feedback-loop model from `MODERN-BUDDHIST-ARGUMENT-feedback-loop.md`), not the traditional
craving/upādāna language. The content is the same; the register is the loop losing its
self-renewal. Sources: the full text of *Nibbāna – The Mind Stilled*, read through the
vortex model.*

---

## THE ONE-LINE ACCOUNT (vortex register)
> **Nibbāna is the feedback loop losing its capacity for self-renewal. Not the destruction
> of the system — the loss of the conditions under which the self-appropriating attractor
> reproduces itself.**

---

## 1. THE LOOP (the vortex, restated)

The saṃsāric loop (from the modern-Buddhist argument):

```
C_t → W_t → V_t → A_t → M_{t+1} → C_{t+1}
```
- C = consciousness
- W = structured phenomenal world
- V = affective valuation (vedanā)
- A = appropriation / action tendency (upādāna)
- M = the resulting self-model that determines subsequent experience

**The loop is self-appropriating:** each pass adds "this is mine" / "this determines what I
am," so the world produced by the loop supplies evidence for the loop. The attractor (the
"I") is regenerated each cycle.

---

## 2. NIBBĀNA = THE LOOP LOSES ITS SELF-RENEWAL (not the craving register)

Ñāṇananda's own images, restated in the loop register:

### The fire / fuel → the self-renewing attractor
Traditional: "existence is a fire kept up by the fuel of grasping."
**Vortex register:** the saṃsāric attractor is maintained by a self-supplied energy term
(the appropriation step A in the loop). Each cycle the system "grasps" the output as its own
input — which is what keeps the attractor in its basin.

**Nibbāna = the attractor losing its self-supplied energy.** The loop no longer feeds
itself. The fire goes out "because the conditions for its existence are no more" — i.e. the
recursive dependency A → M → C is broken. Not the fire destroyed; the fuel no longer
renewed.

### The stilling of all preparations → the loss of self-renewal
Traditional: sabbasaṅkhārasamatha, "the stilling of all preparations."
**Vortex register:** the preparations (saṅkhāra) are the feed-forward terms of the loop —
the shaping of the next state by the current one. Nibbāna = the *stilling* of this
self-preparation: the loop ceases to generate the next condition for its own continuation.

> The machinery can remain active (the projector keeps turning); what ceases is its
> capacity to enclose the system — to make it the renewed object of its own operation.

### The unestablished consciousness → the attractor without its basin
Traditional: "the unestablished consciousness does not find a name-and-form as a reality."
**Vortex register:** the "established" consciousness is one fixed in an attractor basin —
it keeps falling back into the self-appropriating loop. The "unestablished" (anidassana)
consciousness is the state where the basin no longer holds: the loop's output no longer
feeds back as the condition of its own reproduction.

> Established = locked in the attractor basin (the self-model keeps re-forming).
> Unestablished = the basin has lost its grip; the self-model still functions but no longer
> reconstructs the "me"/"mine" organization.

---

## 3. THE BROKEN DEPENDENCY (the sharpest statement)

The vortex model's key insight: liberation is not removing a component — it is **breaking
one dependency** that the whole positive-feedback architecture depends on.

```
V (valuation) ↛ A (appropriation)
```

The first signal (unpleasant vedanā) can occur WITHOUT the loop adding "WHY ME → THIS
SHOULDN'T BE HAPPENING → I NEED THIS TO STOP." If V no longer triggers A, then A no longer
updates M, and M no longer regenerates C — the whole self-appropriating loop loses its
self-renewal.

**Nibbāna = the broken dependency V ↛ A.** Not the destruction of V (vedanā remains), but
the loss of the automatic transition that made the loop self-sustaining.

---

## 4. NIBBĀNA IS AN OPTION, NOT A LOCATION (the register's payoff)

Traditional: nibbāna is not a place or a thing.
**Vortex register:** nibbāna is not another attractor competing with saṃsāra — it is the
*loss of the conditions under which the saṃsāric attractor exists.* The system does not
enter a new state; it stops being re-captured by the old one.

> Nibbāna is the loop incapable of reproducing itself — while the system itself continues
> to function.

---

## 5. THE ARAHANT, IN THE VORTEX REGISTER

The arahant still gets contact → vedanā (the first signal). What no longer happens is the
second-order vortex:

```
unpleasant ↛ "WHY ME" ↛ "THIS SHOULDN'T BE HAPPENING" ↛ future-projection ↛ resistance
```

The system has **stopped recursively manufacturing dukkha from experience.** The signal can
exist without constructing the vortex around it. This is the broken-dependency criterion of
insight: *knowledge is demonstrated by a broken dependency, not by possession of a correct
description.*

---

## 6. THE REFLEXION-ILLUSION (the dog), IN THE VORTEX REGISTER

Ñāṇananda's dog-on-the-plank, restated: the dog's reflexion is a **self-referential loop
that mistakes its own output for an external input.** It sees its own image (its own
reflected state) and treats it as another dog — growling, wagging. The loop feeds itself:
the more it growls, the more it confirms "there's a threat."

**Wisdom (yoniso manasikāra) = breaking the loop by recognizing the output as its own
reflection.** The dog is not fighting another dog; it is fighting the loop's own output
misread as input. Understanding reflection *as* reflection breaks the self-feeding loop.

**This is nibbāna in miniature:** the reflexion-loop loses its self-renewal when its output
is no longer misrecognized as external input.

## 9. THE VORTEX-REGISTER ACCOUNT (one paragraph)
Nibbāna is not a location, a thing, or a state entered after death. It is the **feedback
loop losing its capacity for self-renewal** — the loss of the conditions under which the
self-appropriating attractor (the "I") reproduces itself. The machinery can remain active
(the projector keeps turning, vedanā still arises); what ceases is the loop's power to
enclose the system — the automatic transition from valuation to appropriation (V→A) that
regenerated the "me"/"mine" organization each cycle. When that dependency is broken, the
system no longer recursively manufactures suffering from experience. The reflexion-illusion
(the dog mistaking its own reflection for another dog) is nibbāna's miniature: wisdom is
breaking the self-feeding loop by recognizing its own output as its own. **Nibbāna = the
loop incapable of reproducing itself — while the system itself continues to function.**
