### 1.2 His book on dependent arising: "Fundamental Structure"

Ñāṇavīra's "Note on Fundamental Structure" is his entire book on dependent
arising in miniature. The claim: **paṭiccasamuppāda is not a 12-link chain
across three lives. It is the structure of a single moment of experience.**

The tetrahedron: subject + object + intention + act co-arise. None is prior.
The 12 links are the 12 edges of a star tetrahedron (2 × 6). The principle:

> "When this exists, that comes to be. With the arising of this, that
> arises. When this does not exist, that does not come to be. With the
> cessation of this, that ceases."

### 2.3 What essay1ideas.md missed about Ñāṇananda

1. **The vortex register IS predictive processing.** The C→W→V→A→M loop (consciousness → world → valuation → appropriation → self-model) is a prediction-error minimization loop. Breaking V→A (valuation → appropriation) is breaking the precision-weighting that turns sensation into self. This bridges Ñāṇananda directly to Friston.
2. **The cinema is a film theory.** Ñāṇananda's spectator who "produces the drama himself" via saṅkhāra + āsava is describing exactly what a film audience does. The daylight-flooding-the-cinema image is the film's own ending (wisdom disrupting immersion).
3. **Papañca is the comedy mechanism.** The cactus bit (SEQ 9) doesn't just illustrate papañca — papañca IS the setup. Every setup induces controlled proliferation. The punch is the proliferated world collapsing.

## PART 4: WHAT THE ESSAY STILL DOESN'T COVER

1. **Ñāṇavīra's tetrahedron as film structure.** The dossier treats Ñāṇavīra as a footnote (svasaṃvedana). His fundamental-structure claim — subject/object/intention/act co-arising in a single moment — IS the deep structure of every joke AND every scene. Add a sequence or overlay: each joke = one tetrahedron firing.
2. **Stream-entry as threshold, not gradient.** The film's SEQ 11 treats awakening as gradual weakening. Ñāṇavīra + Hillside say it's a threshold crossing (acceleration stops). The ball animation should show a discontinuity, not a fade.
3. **The Trika-Buddhist tension needs staging.** Tractatus shows Abhinavagupta converts structural reflexivity into universal Self; Ñāṇavīra warns against exactly this. SEQ 12 stages Siva Sutras as "nicer" — the dossier's fact-check is right that this undersells the metaphysical difference. Give the tension a full scene, not a punchline.
4. **Hillside Hermitage original-mind teachings.** 523 videos of Ñāṇavīra-lineage Dhamma, freely available. The "Mind Whisperer" and Right View videos are direct sources for SEQ 9-11. Cite them.
5. **Levin's 2026 error-minimization paper** (2601.14096) postdates the dossier's Levin section. It directly connects embedding-space navigation to predictive processing — the closest scientific parallel to "setup sets model, punch violates, resolution updates."
