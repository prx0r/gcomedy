# 00 — PARAMETERS

## Length
- Target: 25 minutes (1,500 seconds)
- Script budget: ~4,000 spoken words at 160 WPM
- Hard cap: 27 minutes (retention cliff at 25+ for first-time viewers)

## Voice
- AI-generated voice (per owner's note)
- Tone: casual expertise. Fan with a notebook. Not a professor.
- Self-aware: "I've struggled with this as a research assignment"
- Self-deprecating when explaining instead of performing (SEQ 3)
- The voice IS a character — the narrator knows they're making a video

## Visual style
- Dark background (#07070a — Funnier skin palette)
- Red accent (#e8102e) for emphasis
- Gold (#f5c76b) for theory/data overlays
- Inter Tight for body, Anton for titles (Funnier skin fonts)
- Motion graphics: clean, minimal, data-forward. Not busy.
- Live clips: never narrated over a punchline. Play → pause → analyze → resume.

## Pacing
- Cut density: 3-6s normal, 1-2s high energy, 8-12s for compelling unbroken moments
- B-roll ratio: 40% of runtime (Tier 1 purpose-shot where possible, Tier 2 stock, Tier 3 graphics)
- Pattern break every 10-20 seconds: new shot size, insert, example, or caption
- Silence: 2-3s pause after key lines, no music swell. Use sparingly (2-3 times max).
- The 10s opening pause is the film's most extreme silence — earns its place by being the only one that long

## Retention strategy
- Curiosity Loop: mystery (SEQ 1-2) → false answer (SEQ 3: "the brain") → complication (SEQ 4-8) → turn (SEQ 9-10: the self) → resolution (SEQ 11-12) → implication (SEQ 13)
- Mini-payoffs every 3-5 minutes: Happy Birthday resolves, mother joke returns, Autumn Leaves returns
- Never hide all value until the end
- The strongest material escalates: comedy → neuroscience → philosophy → consciousness
- Risk point: SEQ 5 (neuroscience break) — could lose comedy viewers. Mitigate with clips and humor.
- Risk point: SEQ 11 (reincarnation) — could lose philosophy-averse viewers. Mitigate with the meditation experiment (interactive, not lecture).
