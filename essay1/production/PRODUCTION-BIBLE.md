# PRODUCTION BIBLE — Comedy, Music, and the Illusion of Self

> Magnum opus video essay. ~25 minutes. This is the master production document.
> Everything lives under `essay1/production/`. One file per production domain.

## THE FILM IN ONE PARAGRAPH

A comedian creates an imagined world using words. A musician creates one using sounds. Both depend on the audience's brain predicting what happens next. This film demonstrates that mechanism live — through jazz Happy Birthday, Autumn Leaves, Louis CK, Sean Lock, and Carlin — then turns the same mechanism on the audience's sense of self. Using predictive processing, rasa theory, Ñāṇananda's papañca, Levin's bioelectric cognition, and Bhartṛhari's sphoṭa, the film argues that timing is composition, jokes are compression, and the self is the ultimate punchline. The audience arrives thinking it's learning about comedy. It leaves investigating whether the person watching exists.

## STRUCTURE

13 sequences. Curiosity Loop structure (mystery → false answer → complication → turn → resolution → implication). The mystery: what is the most important thing in comedy? The turn: it's the human brain. The implication: the brain constructs the self the same way it constructs a joke's meaning.
