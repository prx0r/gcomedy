## The ascent (each stage = visual + sound + one narration line)

**0 — SILENCE.** Black, 10s. Pure potentiality. Nirvana/0/framework.
*(Built: Scene 00.)*

**0→1 — POINT.** One dot + sine E4. First distinction — but alone it's
nothing yet (Ñāṇavīra: write o a hundred times, still one thing).
Line: "In the beginning, there is a point."

**1→2 — LINE + SECOND POINT.** Two dots (which is 'this'? — §4 problem).
Line drawn between them, labeled "2 semitones" (E→F♯). The line IS the
relation — higher-order thing. **Let Ñāṇavīra explain it here:** o/x,
this/that, priority — in plain words: "Two things. But which comes first?
The difference between them isn't what they're made of. It's which one
you're looking at." Curve into S → yin-yang (each pole seeds the other).

**2→3 — TRIANGLE.** F♯ slides to G (E holds). B enters. E–G–B closes.
First surface. First enclosure in 2D. Autumn Leaves gesture hidden inside
(E…F♯…G). **Vitruvian overlay:** the hidden triangle drawn over the body
(asset exists: essay1/assets/vitruvian-man.jpg) — the body already
containing the geometry.

**3→4 — TETRAHEDRON.** D enters (Em7). Fourth point lifts out of plane.
Six edges. Minimum system (Fuller): smallest thing with inside/outside.
Key established (E minor — all 5 pitch classes, nothing foreign).
Line: "Four points. The smallest thing that can have an inside."

**4→5 — MERKABA (star tetrahedron).** Second tetrahedron interlocks,
pointing down. Up tetra (fire, ascent, Śiva/prakāśa) + down tetra
(water/descent, Śakti/vimarśa). **This is the Vitruvian Man complete:**
circle + square + figure + interlocking triangles. The man standing at the
center of the star is the subject pole co-arisen with the geometric world.
Visual: the annotated Vitruvian asset rotates into 3D, triangles extrude
into tetrahedra, counter-rotate slowly.

**5 — PLATO'S SOLIDS.** Tetrahedron (fire) → cube (earth) → octahedron
(air) → icosahedron (water) → dodecahedron (cosmos/aether). Timaeus
sequence, 10 seconds, morphing one into the next. Sound: each solid gets
its interval (octave 2:1, fifth 3:2, fourth 4:3 — tetractys ratios).
Line: "The Greeks gave each element a shape. Four elements, five solids.
The fifth holds the other four the way the key holds the song."

**6 — THE ONE (Proclus Props 1–6).** Past the solids: "Every manifold is
posterior to the One." Beyond being, beyond knowing, beyond multiplicity —
the condition of all description (Anākhyā, the Undefinable). Visual: all
solids dissolve upward into white, then white dissolves into black. The
One isn't a brighter light — it's the black we started in, recognized.
Line: "Behind every shape, the shapeless. Behind every note, the silence
we started in."

**7 — HENADS = ANGELS (Proclus Props 7–24).** "Every god is a self-complete
henad" (Prop 114). The ones: irreducible unities mediating between the One
and multiplicity. **Angels, reframed:** not winged messengers but
theurgical subsidiaries — the units of divine action the theurgist works
with to ascend. Each henad a complete configuration of consciousness, all
the same consciousness. Visual: white points differentiating out of the
black like stars, each labeled only by number. Sound: chord tones entering
one by one (angelic choir as additive synthesis — each voice a sine).
Line: "The One is too simple to touch the world directly. So it touches
it through ones. The old books called them gods. Later books called them
angels. They are the steps on the ladder, and the ladder is made of the
same thing as the top."

**8 — PROCESSION + RETURN (Props 25–39 = spanda).** monē (remaining) →
proodos (procession) → epistrophē (return). Identical to sṛṣṭi-sthiti-
saṃhāra. The whole ascent (0→7) replays in reverse in 10 seconds:
angels → solids → tetrahedron → triangle → line → point → black. The film
demonstrates return right after demonstrating procession. Line: "Everything
that goes out comes back. That's not poetry, that's the structure."

**9 — AMPLITUHEDRON (modern coda, 20 seconds).** Arkani-Hamed: scattering
amplitudes as volumes of positive geometry — locality + unitarity emerge,
not assumed. Coherence, resonance, positivity as physics. The One's
superabundance (Proclus: procession without diminishment — the source
loses nothing by overflowing) stated in 2020s physics language: abundance
is structural, not miraculous. Visual: amplituhedron polytope rotating
(open-access visuals, attribute). Line: "Physicists found the same thing
last decade, in particle collisions. They called it positivity. The
universe computes its own probabilities out of pure shape."
