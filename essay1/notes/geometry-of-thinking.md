## Fuller's literal claim
*Synergetics* is subtitled "Explorations in the Geometry of Thinking" —
meant literally: concepts constrained by the same topology as 3D
sphere-packing. Minimum system = tetrahedron (4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 faces;
Syn 400.10, 402.02). Key sections: 905.01, 265.09 ("conceptuality is
tetrahedral"), 267.01–267.04 (observer as tetrasystem), 227.01 (abacus),
400.10–400.44.

## What's solid (use freely)
- **Minimum system theorem:** 4 points = smallest enclosure with
  inside/outside (3 = surface, 2 = line, 1 = point, 0 = nothing).
- **Identity in relations, not components:** 6 edges make the tetrahedron,
  not 4 vertices. Pattern integrity through material turnover (Levin's
  planaria confirmed experimentally).
- **"One plus one equals four"** (108.03): two triangles associated as
  helices yield a tetrahedron — synergy, the whole unpredicted by parts.
- **Tetrahelix:** face-to-face chaining spirals indefinitely — minimum
  system scales to infinite structure.
- **36 tattvas = 9 tetrahedra** (tantraloka-tetrahedron.md): ladder as
  chain of shared vertices. Four speech levels (parā/paśyantī/madhyamā/
  vaikharī) as tetrahedron with 6 edges — every utterance traverses all 6.

## What's NOT solid (do not claim on screen)
Per the volume's own geometryofthinking transcript (verdict, 6 points):
1. Fuller never empirically demonstrated cognition is tetrahedral.
2. K₄ combinatorics is real math; "all thought is tetrahedral" doesn't follow.
3. The four derivations (Fuller/Ñāṇavīra/Gowan/Friston) do NOT derive the
   same structure — shared cardinality ≠ structural identity.
4. **The 36-convergence has an arithmetic error:** cumulative pairs at N=7
   is 56, not 36. (36 = pairs among nine.) Do not cite the 36/36 match.
5. Viable hypothesis is narrower: four-role relational structures MAY
   SOMETIMES form 3-simplex representations. Testable, untested.

## Film consequences
- Claim on screen: "four points is the smallest thing with an inside"
  (solid). Never claim: "thought is tetrahedral" (unproven), the 36/36
  match (arithmetic error), or that Ñāṇavīra + Fuller derived the same
  thing (they didn't — one derives enclosure, the other reflexive
  transformation structure).
- The tetrahedron-as-selector proof (density/) is aspirational formal work,
  not established result — background, not narration.
- Jitterbug (VE→icosa→octa→tetra collapse) is a REAL geometric sequence —
  usable as the coda visual (world compressing back toward silence)
  without any cognitive claims attached.
