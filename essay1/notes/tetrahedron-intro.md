> Workshopped 2026-10-18. Status: design approved by owner, to be written
> into voiceover-00 + screenplay SEQ 0. **Title locked: THE GEOMETRY OF COMEDY.**

## The progression (one continuous 60–90s shot)
0. **BLACK/SILENCE** (10s). Nirvana, pure potentiality, framework with no things.
1. **POINT.** Single dot + single sine tone (E4). First distinction — but a
   point alone is nothing yet (Ñāṇavīra: write o a hundred times, still one
   thing). Narration whisper: "In the beginning, there is a point."
2. **SECOND POINT + LINE.** Two dots (which is 'this'? — §4 problem staged).
   Line drawn between them, labeled "2 semitones" (E→F♯). The line IS the
   relation/operation, higher-order thing. Curve into S: yin-yang (setup
   contains punch's seed and vice versa).
3. **TRIANGLE.** F♯ slides a semitone to G (E holds as common tone). Third
   dot on B. E–G–B: E minor triad closes. **The F♯→G slide IS the opening
   gesture of Autumn Leaves** — melody begins before the audience knows it.
   AAB is a triangle: two similar events (base), third breaks it.
4. **TETRAHEDRON.** Fourth dot lifts out of plane; +D. E–G–B–D = Em7, the
   tonic system. Six edges draw; label two (semitone move, fifth frame).
   Four poles = inside/outside now exist (Fuller minimum system). In sound:
   the key. Narration, one line: "Four points. The smallest thing that can
   have an inside."
5. **DISSOLVE → the head.** Tetrahedron dissolves to black; E keeps ringing.
   Band enters with Autumn Leaves head on the same E. The song RESULTS, it
   doesn't start. Then: "What is the most important thing in comedy?"

## Musical design (honest Tymoczko, E minor)
- Conjunct motion: everything arrives by step (F♯→G) or common tone (E held).
- Consonance: triad (stable) → seventh (color).
- Harmonic consistency: 5 pitch classes, all E natural minor. Nothing foreign.
- Limited macroharmony: 90 seconds inside one scale.
- Centricity: E sounds nearly continuously.
- Timbre follows geometry: sine → two sines → warm triangle wave → full band.
- Comedy rides free: silence→premise (point) → setup+expectation (line) →
  punch (triangle closes) → evening's world (tetrahedron). Zero explanation.

## Structural payoffs
- The intro contains the ending: tetrahedron dissolves to black = coda black
  reads as homecoming (third black), not emptiness. Round trip 0→world→0.
- Plato's One → dyad → many in 90 seconds (nod, don't lecture).
- Fuller's pattern integrity pre-pays laptop + self (identity =
  relation-structure, not substance).
