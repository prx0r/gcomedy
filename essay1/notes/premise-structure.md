# NOTES — Premise as shared structure (Ñāṇavīra grounding)

> Workshopped 2026-10-18. Status: thinking, feeds voiceover-00 prologue.

## The claim
A premise isn't the setup. It's the shared framework within which setup and
punch can appear at all — the condition for priority ('this' vs 'that') to
exist. Without an accepted premise there is no foreground/background, only
noise. Rock's rule (nothing works until the premise is understood) follows:
no shared framework → no priority → operations have nothing to transform.

## Structural jobs of a premise (from Fundamental Structure)
1. **Creates the possibility space.** "Autumn Leaves in D minor" = the notes
   that can appear. Like o/x: the premise declares the symbols in play.
2. **Sets initial priority.** One reading becomes 'this', the rest 'that'.
   The punch promotes a 'that' to 'this' — the promotion IS the laugh.
3. **Establishes directedness.** Experience is intentional; it points. A
   premise points the audience (cetanā at aesthetic level). AAB needs a
   pointed A; random sentences fail for lack of vector, not lack of weirdness.
4. **Constitutes its own audience.** Subject and object co-arise. Accepting
   the premise makes the audience-member (subject pole) co-arise with the
   shared world (object). The premise manufactures its participants.
   "Leave your world, enter this one" = dropping one subject-position so a
   new one can co-arise. The prologue visibly manufactures its audience —
   leave that legible.
5. **Starts weight distribution.** Wide at acceptance, narrowed by setup
   beats (squaring toward the dominant reading), collapsed by the punch.
6. **Nests hierarchically.** Bit ⊂ set ⊂ evening ⊂ comedy ⊂ film's stated
   premise. Self-similar levels. Stating the top level (reflexion with the
   same structure as the immediate) frames rather than breaks.

## The punch as cessation
Run dependent origination through a joke: contact (premise lands) → feeling
(tone) → perception ("it's a Monopoly story") → directedness → farmed
proliferation. Then the punch severs the expected continuation: "when this
does not exist, that does not come to be" — the expected future ceases, and
dependent on that cessation the laugh arises. Predicts the inverted-U (too
little built = nothing to sever; too much locked = severance reads as error).
