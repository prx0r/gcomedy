**SCRIPT:**
"This is how your self works. At no point have you ever been a laptop. You
are a collection of parts.

[Levin imagery builds: cells → tissues → organs → organism]

Cells pursue goals. Tissues maintain organs. Organs serve organisms.
There may be no single scale where agency begins.

Henri Bergson — comedy theory. Memory. Aristotle — memory. Callbacks. A
callback works because your brain holds the past and the present at once.

And maybe comedy can be related to information theory. Because you're
basically taking ones and zeros and compressing them into a packet — then
delivering that packet to another body so they can understand it. The
laughter is feedback on how clear the compression was.

The most entertaining outcome is the one you didn't expect — yet somehow
accept. Because you're wired for novelty. New information means growth.
Means survival."

skeletons → organs → cells → bioelectric gradients). Bergson/Aristotle as

packets, checksum tick).

figures + DotGrid/Boids cell field). Bioelectric imagery: real Levin lab
images where CC-available, procedural gradients otherwise (honest label:
"illustration" in corner).

## SCENE 16 — REBIRTH + VORTEX + MEDITATION (block 36b + 37, ~4 min)

**SCRIPT:**
"Well — in every moment, you are being reborn.

This is what nirvana is. It is saying: right now, and only right now,
there is a path you can follow to stop rebirth. The logic is: you must
already be at rest in nirvana. It must always be the case. Yet you are
continually reborn in different mental states, and you do not realise it.

You are under the complete illusion that something outside of mentality —
outside of consciousness — exists.

Rebirth is being born as an identity persisting through time. And dying.
Having a thought — is rebirth.

The end of rebirth is when the possibility of being reborn in these
different mental states becomes impossible. Because there is no grasping
mechanism left. And grasping is the cause of looking outwards.

[VISUAL: vortex animation — feedback loop, momentum arrows]

Talk about the vortex. Momentum carries on by itself. Karma IS that
momentum. Practice is reversing the momentum.

[VISUAL: ball rolling downhill (accelerating) vs ball on flat ground
(winding down). Stream entry: the threshold where acceleration stops.]

Practice is the stilling of the mind — for a second. Every second you do
it, you weaken the current. This is called meditation.

If you want to actually meditate, here's the secret formula. Commit one
hundred percent. Say: okay, for the next one minute, my sole intention is
to have no thoughts. Then see what happens.

[60-SECOND TIMER. Thoughts arise on screen as text fragments, unbidden.
No narration. The audience meditates — or fails to — live.]

You will find that an image enters your mind. Or a memory. And you will
see that you can't actually control whether you can have a thought or not.
Further — you may see that you have never actually seen a thought. Yet you
know what I am talking about.

A thought is a possession of awareness.

So a better meditation instruction might be: do not get possessed in the
next one minute. Do everything you can not to get infiltrated.

This is the school of Maha Boowa. He says: once you stop getting
infiltrated, finally the true nature of the self is revealed — as the
consciousness that was always present.

Rebirth is your awareness being hijacked by various influences — deceiving
you into believing there is something to be attained. It's like the beggar
sitting on a pot of gold, suffering of starvation.

[VISUAL: beggar illustration]

Until the momentum is weakened enough, you reach a threshold called stream
entry. That is basically where the acceleration has stopped, and the
process is now winding down."

**VISUAL DIRECTION:** Vortex (FlowField, redshift with momentum).
Ball physics (two balls, side by side, one accelerating downhill / one
decelerating on flat — real physics sim, not keyframed). Timer UI (60s,
thought-fragments spawn at random positions, fade). Beggar (single
illustration, hold 5s).

**TECH:** Vortex: shaders FlowField + momentum arrows (canvas overlay).
Ball sim: matter.js via Monet Canvas patterns, or hand-rolled kinematics
(v² = u² + 2as vs exponential decay — REAL equations on screen, teardown
rule: every number measured or derived). Timer: HyperFrames composition,
trivially deterministic.

**FISH VOICE DIRECTION:** louis, dynamic range is widest here: 150 WPM for
rebirth exposition → 120 WPM for vortex/mechanics → 130 WPM intimate for
the meditation instruction ("here's the secret formula" — shared secret
energy, leaning in) → SILENCE for 60s (no voice at all) → 125 WPM,
gentle, for the debrief (possession/infiltration — don't perform fear,
perform clarity) → 140 WPM rising hope for beggar → 120 WPM, finality,
for stream entry.

## SCENE 17 — HELL REALMS / KARMA (block 38, ~2 min)

**SCRIPT:**
"Being reborn in the hell realms is equal to feeling the emotion of anger.
And karma is what happens when you take action from that emotion, and it
manifests into actuality.

Imagine it like this. Imagine your whole life you have responded with
violence when in an argument. You get into a disagreement with your child.
And you get violent with them. And you take action.

Now you suffer the consequences of a lifetime of training your habitual
system to respond in this way — with a fateful action that seemed out of
your control. Because indeed it was. Yet the seed was planted long ago. By
your parents. By their parents. Watered by you. Coming to fruition in a
fateful moment, when conditions ripened — and you finally learn the lesson.

In this moment, you have a brief window to change.

[2s black]"

**VISUAL DIRECTION:** No clips. No jokes. Dark red gradient (raudra rasa
color — fury — the film using its own rasa system). Habit chain animation:
parent → parent → you → child, seeds watering, fruit ripening (botanical
imagery for karma — growth, not punishment). The violence itself is NEVER
shown (restraint is the point — the film refuses the cheap image). "Brief
window" — a literal window of light opens in the dark for 2s, then the
black.

**TECH:** Gradient + botanical chain animation (canvas). Window: simple
mask reveal.

**FISH VOICE DIRECTION:** louis, 120 WPM throughout, heaviest scene in the
film. No performance ticks, no asides, no mother jokes. "By your parents.
By their parents." — flat recitation, let the words do it. "A brief window
to change" — the only lift in the scene, quiet hope, then black. The
audience needs this scene to be serious so the Siva Sutras release lands.
