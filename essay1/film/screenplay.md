## SCENE 03 — The Laptop Experiment (SEQ 10, ~90s in full film)

**Narration (louis_reference_lab):**
"Look at the laptop you're watching this on. That's a laptop. Congratulations,
your brain works. [beat] Remove the screen. Is it still a laptop? Remove the
keyboard. The battery. The processor. Which part was the laptop? [beat] At no
point in its existence has it ever truly been a laptop."

**Visual intent:** Closed laptop → screen opens → six layers explode in a wave
→ gold labels draw per layer → parts ghost out while the word "laptop" hangs
solid → snap reassemble to the opening framing. Nothing changed, everything
changed. The word floats; the object dissolves. Designation vs essence, shown
not said.

**Pacing:** Slow orbit during recognition (imaginary metronome). Hard cuts on
beats during explosion. Two deliberate silences: after "your brain works"
(2s) and after "truly been a laptop" (black, 3s).

**References:** anatomy desk-computer pattern, lemo-opuscar teardown
(21 Keys, Opened), pc-anatomy auto-disassemble.
