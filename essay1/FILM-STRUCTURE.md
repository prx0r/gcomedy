# FILM STRUCTURE — Comedy, Music, and the Illusion of Self

> Built from essayhuman.md + every trialled repo. High-level themes first,
> then the visual language system, then per-sequence plans, then the two
> hero sequences designed shot-by-shot (laptop + Levin body).

## HIGH-LEVEL THEMES (3 layers, one film)

The film operates on three layers simultaneously. Every sequence lives on
one layer primarily and gestures at the next.

**Layer 1 — CRAFT (SEQ 1–6): how comedy and music are built.**
Timing is composition. The setup is a prediction. The punch is a violation.
Callbacks are harmonic returns. Delivery is voicing. This layer is
demonstrable, measurable, and funny. The audience learns the machinery.

**Layer 2 — MIND (SEQ 7–10): how the machinery reveals the mind.**
The same prediction machinery that makes jokes land also constructs
perception, language, memory, and identity. Abhinavagupta (rasa),
Bhartṛhari (sphoṭa), Bergson (rigidity), Ñāṇananda (papañca) each show one
facet: the mind builds worlds out of predictions, then mistakes the worlds
for reality. The laptop is the hinge object.

**Layer 3 — SELF (SEQ 11–13): the machinery turned on the audience.**
Moment-to-moment rebirth. The vortex losing self-renewal. Stream entry as
threshold. The self as the ultimate punchline — the prediction the brain
makes about who is predicting. The film's structure IS the argument:
setup (comedy) → escalation (mind) → punchline (self).

## VISUAL LANGUAGE SYSTEM

One system across the whole film. No sequence invents its own grammar.

**Palette (Funnier skin):** bg `#07070a`, text `#f3f1ec`, red `#e8102e`
(emphasis, violation, punch), gold `#f5c76b` (theory, data, timeless),
muted `#8d8b93` (setup, ordinary). Rasa overlays tint per flavor when
rasa theory is on screen (hasya = warm amber, raudra = red, shanta = cool grey).

**Type:** Anton for titles/numbers, Inter Tight for body. Kinetic type for
punchlines (word-by-word reveal synced to narration). Phone-minimum sizes
from dissect field guide.

**Motion grammar (from video-shotcraft cards + teardown style):**
- Expectation = slow orbit / steady dolly (the imaginary metronome).
- Violation = hard cut or whip-pan on the beat (never a dissolve for a punch).
- Return/callback = match-cut to the earlier framing (same angle, new context).
- Dissolve/particles = ontological transitions ONLY (laptop→parts,
  body→cells→charge). Never use dissolves for jokes; never use hard cuts
  for dissolutions. The transition TYPE carries meaning.

**Shader vocabulary (shaders repo, deterministic):**
- `NoiseDissolve` / `BlockDissolve` — object → components.
- `ContourLines` / `DotGrid` — measurement/analysis overlays.
- `FlowField` / `CurlNoise` / `FloatingParticles` — currents, vortices,
  bioelectric fields, the Ñāṇananda whirlpool.
- `Aurora` — spanda/consciousness backgrounds (joecroskey aurora refs).
- `PagePeel` — chapter transitions (layers of self peeled back).

**Sound grammar (earshot + Fish):**
- Narration: our 7 voices (louis for storytelling, seinfeld for process).
  Word timestamps drive per-word visuals.
- Foley: earshot `sfx` bank, placed by measured peak. Clicks/hinges for
  laptop, swells for vortex, silence for black-screen beats.
- `earshot critique` on every finished sequence before assembly.

shows mechanisms, not consciousness itself. Levin = how agencies integrate;
Trika = what consciousness is. Say so on screen in one gold line. The
dissolves are honest about what they show (structure) vs what they don't
(the hard problem).

## THEMES AS PRODUCTION RULES

1. **The transition type IS the philosophy.** Hard cuts = jokes/violations.
   Dissolves = ontological change. Never cross them.
2. **Every number on screen is measured or sourced.** Teardown rule:
   FACTS.md for every claim. No invented stats.
3. **The film demonstrates, then explains.** Happy Birthday first, theory
   after. Laptop first, emptiness after. Never lecture before showing.
4. **Callbacks are structural, not decorative.** Mother jokes, Autumn
   Leaves, Happy Birthday each return at the moment the film explains
   callbacks. The form teaches itself.
5. **Silence is the rarest effect.** 10s open, 2–3s mid-film (×2 max),
   final black. Everything else moves.
