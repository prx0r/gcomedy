## PREDICTIVE PROCESSING & FREE ENERGY (the theoretical spine)

### Koelsch, Vuust & Friston — "Predictive Processes and the Peculiar Case of Music" (2018, TiCS)
*The* framing paper. Music violates predictions we enjoy — we seek "predictably surprising" events (high information gain). Under active inference, this resolves uncertainty — an "epistemic offering." Musical tension = predictions, their precision, violation, and fulfillment.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The setup is the prediction. The punch is the violation. Comedy offers the same epistemic offering: you *want* the surprise because resolving uncertainty is pleasurable.

### Friston & Friston — "A Free Energy Formulation of Music Generation and Perception: Helmholtz Revisited" (2013)
The brain as Helmholtz machine: constructs predictions, minimizes surprise. Predictive processing is fundamental to music in three ways: (1) temporal dynamics, (2) interaction/synchronization, (3) emotional/aesthetic effects. Exactly the three axes comedy uses too.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The formal bridge — both comedy and music are prediction-error minimization systems that deliberately introduce error for epistemic value.

### Friston — "The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?" (2010, Nat Rev Neurosci)
The original. Free energy = upper bound on surprise. Agents minimize free energy = minimize surprise. Organisms maintain themselves in limited sensory states (low entropy).
**Use:** Chapter 2 endnote + Chapter 13 (conservation laws). The room's energy system in party mode is a free-energy system: laughs lower surprise (melt resistance), boos raise it.

### Clark — "A nice surprise? Predictive processing and the active pursuit of the unexpected" (2017, Synthese)
Why do we seek surprise if we minimize surprise? Resolves the paradox: epistemic foraging — we seek the *right kind* of surprise (novelty that reduces uncertainty about the world model). Precision-weighted prediction errors drive exploration.
**Use:** Chapter 3. Why random humor fails (no structure = no uncertainty to resolve) and why structured surprise works (uncertainty exists, then gets resolved).

### Cheung et al. — "Uncertainty and Surprise Jointly Predict Musical Pleasure" (2019, Current Biology)
**The killer paper.** Pleasure is NOT linear in surprise — it's the interaction of uncertainty (prospective) and surprise (retrospective). High pleasure = high surprise predicted with low uncertainty OR low surprise predicted with high uncertainty. Amygdala + hippocampus + auditory cortex carry the interaction. Nucleus accumbens carries uncertainty only.
**Use:** Chapter 2 + Chapter 14 (composing). This is the punchline equation: a joke is funny when the setup makes the wrong interpretation highly certain (low uncertainty), then the punch delivers high surprise. Or when the setup is ambiguous (high uncertainty) and the punch is a clean resolution (low surprise). Both are funny. Neither alone is.

### Gold et al. — "Predictability and Uncertainty in the Pleasure of Music: A Reward for Learning?" (2019, J Neurosci)
Quadratic (inverted-U) effects of information content and entropy on liking. Intermediate complexity is optimal. Preferences for more predictability during more uncertain contexts — facilitates uncertainty reduction. Repeating stimuli decreases liking but doesn't disrupt the intermediate-complexity preference.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The inverted-U of comedy: too familiar = boring, too random = confusing. Intermediate complexity = funniest. Also explains why callbacks work: they reduce uncertainty (you know the reference) while maintaining surprise (new voicing).

### Gold et al. — "Musical reward prediction errors engage the nucleus accumbens and motivate learning" (2019, PNAS)
Music elicits formally modeled reward prediction errors (RPEs) in NAc, same as food/money. Music acts as a reward driving learning. RPEs explain variance in individual learning rates.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The laugh IS a reward prediction error. The room learns from each joke which predictions will be violated and how. This is why comedy is a learning system for the audience's world model.

### Ishida & Nittono — "Active Inference in Music Perception: Motor Engagement to Syncopation Modulates Rhythmic Prediction Error" (2025, Psychophysiology)
Tapping along to syncopation improves meter precision (larger MMN), reduces surprisal. Groove = active inference: the body moves to resolve prediction error. Pleasure from movement only in tapping condition.
**Use:** Chapter 6. The audience isn't passive — they entrain. Laughter synchrony is the comedic equivalent of tapping along: the body resolves the prediction error of the joke by laughing.

### Salimpoor et al. — "Anatomically distinct dopamine release during anticipation and experience of peak emotion to music" (2011, Nat Neurosci)
Dopamine in caudate during anticipation, nucleus accumbens during peak experience. Two distinct temporal phases. Anticipation of abstract reward = dopamine in distinct pathway from the pleasure itself.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The setup (anticipation, caudate) and punch (experience, NAcc) are neurochemically distinct — not one event but two. Comedy inherits this two-phase dopamine architecture.

### Mas-Herrero et al. — "Unraveling the Temporal Dynamics of Reward Signals in Music-Induced Pleasure with TMS" (2021, J Neurosci)
Causal (not just correlational): TMS over dlPFC modulates musical pleasure bidirectionally. NAcc activity predicts both hedonic and motivational responses. Fronto-striatal pathway is indispensable.
**Use:** Endnote. Causal evidence that the prediction-reward circuit isn't just correlated with music pleasure — it IS the mechanism.

### Ferreri et al. — "Dopamine modulates the reward experiences elicited by music" (2019, PNAS)
Pharmacological: levodopa increases musical pleasure, risperidone decreases it. Dopamine causally mediates musical reward. Bidirectional.
**Use:** Endnote. The chemistry of pleasure is shared across domains — music and comedy both run on dopamine prediction-error signaling.

## HUMOR NEUROSCIENCE

### Vrticka, Black & Reiss — "The neural basis of humour processing" (2013, Nat Rev Neurosci)
Core humor network: temporo-occipito-parietal areas (incongruity detection + resolution) + mesocorticolimbic dopaminergic system + amygdala (reward + salience). Personality and sex differences modulate the network.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The humor brain reuses the same dopaminergic prediction-error system as music. Different cortical entry (TOPJ vs auditory cortex), same reward exit.

### Nakamura et al. — "The role of the amygdala in incongruity resolution" (2017, Soc Cogn Affect Neurosci)
fMRI, 51 participants. Separated incongruity detection from resolution using "Given A, I'd say B, because C" frame. Left amygdala activates during resolution (not detection). Amygdala = emotional evaluation + relevance detection.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The amygdala resolves the incongruity — it's the same region that processes musical tension resolution. The punchline lands in the amygdala.

### Sanada, Kumagai & Katayama — "The resolution stage, not the incongruity detection stage, is related to the subjective feeling of humor" (2022, Brain Research)
ERP study with Japanese nazokake puns. N400 = incongruity detection. P600 = resolution. Funny puns have larger P600, same N400. **Resolution, not detection, is what makes something funny.**
**Use:** Chapter 2. Critical distinction: incongruity alone isn't funny — resolution of incongruity is. This is why random humor fails and why the benign-violation constraint matters.

### Chan & Lavallee — "Temporo-parietal and fronto-parietal lobe contributions to theory of mind and executive control in verbal jokes" (2015)
Three joke types activate different networks: bridging-inference (TPJ, theory of mind), exaggeration (IPL/IFG, executive control), ambiguity (IPL/IFG, retrieval). Common core: dlPFC (script-shifting) + vmPFC (appreciation).
**Use:** Chapter 10. Different joke types are different computational problems — not one geometry but a family of geometries sharing a reward system.

### Tian et al. — "Getting the Joke: Insight during Humor Comprehension" (2017, Front Psychol)
fMRI: insight-during-humor shares brain areas with insight-during-problem-solving (STG, MTG, IFG, TPJ, hippocampus). The "aha" of a punchline is the same neural event as the "aha" of a solution.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The punchline is a solution to the setup's problem. Comedy is insight, timed for social delivery.

### Why Clowns Taste Funny (2017, PMC6485420)
fMRI on puns. Left ITG + IFG process semantic ambiguity. Jokes (vs non-jokes with ambiguity) activate amygdala + ventral striatum + midbrain. Funniness ratings correlate with midbrain + striatum activity. Puns keep both meanings alive — ambiguity resolution doesn't collapse to one reading.
**Use:** Chapter 5 (harmony). The pun is a superposition — two interpretations held simultaneously, like a chord. The humor is in the superposition, not the collapse.

## MUSIC REWARD & PLEASURE

### Zatorre & Salimpoor — "From perception to pleasure: Music and its neural substrates" (2013, PNAS)
Review: auditory cortex (STG) + frontal cortex = prediction loop; nucleus accumbens = reward. Functional connectivity between NAcc and STG increases with reward value. Different people like different music = different prior experiences shaping the prediction loop.
**Use:** Chapter 10. Persona = prior experience. A character's "key" is their accumulated prediction loop — the same joke resonates differently for different characters because their STG-NAcc connectivity is trained on different material.

### Gebauer, Kringelbach & Vuust — "Ever-changing cycles of musical pleasure: The role of dopamine and anticipation" (2012, Psychomusicology)
Pleasure cycle: wanting/expectation → liking → learning. Dopamine represents expectation and prediction error (wanting/learning), not liking directly. Music can be pleasurable when it fulfills OR violates expectations.
**Use:** Chapter 2. The comedy pleasure cycle: want the punch (setup) → get the punch (violation + resolution) → learn the pattern (callback economy). Three phases, dopamine in the first and third.

### Witek et al. — "Pleasure and rhythmic syncopation" (2014, PLOS ONE)
Moderate syncopation = maximum pleasure + desire to move. Inverted-U. Funk drum breaks. Not too predictable, not too random.
**Use:** Chapter 6. The rhythm of a bit: moderate syncopation (unexpected punchline placement) maximizes pleasure. Too straight = boring. Too syncopated = unlistenably. Same for timing.

## AAB PATTERN (music + humor shared structure)

### Rozin, Rozin, Appel & Wachtel — "Documenting and Explaining the Common AAB Pattern in Music and Humor" (2006, Emotion)
Two similar events + one dissimilar. Prevalent in music genres and repetitive jokes. Experimentally: AAB maximally effective for humor response. Minimum two iterations to establish pattern; once established, prompt violation is maximally efficient.
**Use:** Chapter 1 (opening). The foundational evidence that music and humor share compositional structure. AAB = the minimal setup-punch unit: establish, reinforce, transform.

## BENIGN VIOLATION THEORY

### McGraw & Warren — "Benign Violation Theory" (2010, Humor Research Lab)
Three conditions: (1) violation, (2) benign, (3) simultaneous. Integrates incongruity + superiority + relief theories. Explains failures: too tame (no violation) or too risky (not benign).
**Use:** Chapter 6 (target field). The benign-violation manifold: every joke is a point in (violation × benignity) space. The manifold constrains which violations are playable from which positions. Stepping off the manifold = set dies.

### Warren & McGraw — "Differentiating What Is Humorous From What Is Not" (2016, JPSP)
Six studies: incongruity alone doesn't differentiate funny from non-funny. Benign violation does. Reduces false positives (joyous/tragic/amazing situations predicted funny by incongruity alone).
**Use:** Chapter 6. The gate: violation + distance + benignity, checked before a joke ships. The jokegate edgy/edge tests are implementations of this.

### McGraw — "A brief introduction to the benign violation theory of humor" (2010, petermcgraw.org)
Three ways to make violation benign: (1) lack of commitment to the norm, (2) distance from the violation, (3) alternative interpretation. Tickling = physically threatening but harmless (mock attack).
**Use:** Chapter 6. The three benignity dials: commitment, distance, alternative-frame. A comedian's stance in idea-space sets all three. Non-human personas (greys, reptilians) get distance for free — "non-humans can't be canceled" (the jokegate EDGY.md thesis).

## COMPUTATIONAL HUMOR (measuring the geometry)

### Xie — "Uncertainty and Surprisal Jointly Deliver the Punchline" (2020, arXiv 2012.12007)
GPT-2 models setup uncertainty + punchline surprisal. Both features outperform baselines for humor recognition. Setup = developing semantic uncertainty. Punchline = disrupting expectations. Joint model best.
**Use:** Chapter 14 (composing). The measurable punchline equation: uncertainty(setup) × surprisal(punchline) → funniness score. Directly implementable — this is the orbifold distance constraint for comedy.

### Bereta et al. — "A Computational Model of Linguistic Humor in Puns" (2016, PLoS Comput Biol)
Noisy-channel model of sentence processing. Ambiguity (entropy over meanings) + distinctiveness (symmetrized KL divergence between meaning-supporting word distributions) predict funniness. Ambiguity distinguishes puns from non-puns; distinctiveness distinguishes funny puns from mediocre ones.
**Use:** Chapter 5 (harmony). The pun geometry: ambiguity = how many interpretations are live. Distinctiveness = how separate the interpretation-supporting evidence is. High ambiguity + high distinctiveness = the funniest puns. This is a chord with two distant but well-supported voicings.

## JAZZ IMPROVISATION NEUROSCIENCE (improv = the party game)

### Limb & Braun — "Neural Substrates of Spontaneous Musical Performance: An fMRI Study of Jazz Improvisation" (2008, PLoS ONE)
**The classic.** Improvisation = deactivation of lateral PFC (dlPFC, LOFC: self-monitoring) + focal activation of medial PFC (frontal polar: self-expression). Sensorimotor activation + limbic deactivation. Hypofrontality = flow state.
**Use:** Chapter 12 (improv geometry). Crowd work / riffing = same hypofrontality. The comedian in flow: self-monitoring off, self-expression on, limbic quiet. This is why improv feels effortless — it IS, neurally.

### Vergara et al. — "Functional network connectivity during Jazz improvisation" (2021, Sci Rep)
Improvisation = weak connectivity between ECN and DMN (attenuated executive control). Flow = unhindered creation. Same whether performed or imagined (vocalized).
**Use:** Chapter 12. Improvisation is a connectivity state, not an activation state. The party-game agent needs the same: loose coupling between evaluation and generation.

### Rosen et al. — "Creative flow as optimized processing" (2024, Neuropsychologia)
EEG during jazz improv. High-flow = transient hypofrontality + reduced posterior DMN in experts. Low-expertise: no flow-related modulation. Flow = optimized domain-specific processing enabled by practice + reduced cognitive control.
**Use:** Chapter 12. Flow is expertise-dependent. The comedian's "years of stage time" = the neural automation that permits hypofrontality. Characters with more sets/minutes = more automated = more flow-capable.

### Limb et al. — "Connecting to Create: Expertise in Musical Improvisation" (2014, J Neurosci)
39 pianists. Improvisation hours → reduced frontoparietal activity + increased fronto-premotor connectivity. Training automates creative behavior at the network level. Specific to improvisation, not classical training.
**Use:** Chapter 12. Career stats as neural efficiency. A character's party_records (sets played, wins) = the training data that automates their creative network.

### Beaty — "The neuroscience of musical improvisation" (2015, Neurosci Biobehav Rev)
Review: pre-SMA, MPFC, IFG, dlPFC, dorsal premotor = the improvisation network. Cooperation between cognitive control and spontaneous thought. Pressing's theoretical model.
**Use:** Chapter 12 endnote. The network is shared across domains — freestyle rap activates the same regions. Comedy improv is in the family.

### Alves Da Mota et al. — "Creativity in Music: The Brain Dynamics of Jazz Improvisation" (2025, Eur J Neurosci)
LEiDA analysis: improvisation = brain substate dominated by auditory + sensorimotor + posterior salience. Free improvisation = substate including DMN + ECN + language. Higher freedom = more notes, more melodic entropy, less pitch predictability.
**Use:** Chapter 12. The improv spectrum: constrained improv (melody changes) vs free improv (chord changes) = different brain states. Party games are constrained improv — the format is the chord changes, the entry is the improvisation.

## RASA THEORY (Indian aesthetics — the tonality map)

### Abhinavagupta — Abhinavabharati (c. 10th century CE) + Natya Shastra (Bharata, c. 200 BCE–200 CE)
Nine rasas: shringara (erotic), hasya (comic), karuna (compassionate), raudra (furious), veera (heroic), bhayanaka (terrifying), bibhatsa (odious), adbhuta (wondrous), shanta (peaceful). Rasa = flavor/juice/sap. Produced from vibhava (determinants) + anubhava (consequents) + vyabhicari (transitory states). Bhava → rasa = emotion → aesthetic relish. The spectator becomes a "taster" (rasika).
**Use:** Chapter 4. The nine rasas = the tonal centers of comedy. Hasya is never alone — it's always hasya *inflected* by another rasa. This is limited macroharmony: a comedian's range is the set of rasa-combinations they can voice without modulating away from their center.

### Higgins — "An Alchemy of Emotion: Rasa and Aesthetic Breakthroughs" (2007, J Aesthetics Art Crit)
Rasa = aesthetic breakthrough, not mere emotional response. The spectator transcends personal emotion to savor universalized flavor. Abhinavagupta links rasa to camatkāra (wonder) and ultimately to spiritual liberation (shanta). Schopenhauer drew on Indian aesthetics → influenced Nietzsche's Birth of Tragedy.
**Use:** Chapter 5 (camatkāra). The laugh is a mini-camatkāra: consciousness recognizing its own freedom in the structure of the violation. Rasa theory is the oldest neuroscience of aesthetic pleasure — and it predicted the modern finding that resolution (not detection) is what produces the experience.

### Mukhopadhyay — "Rasa theory and neural mechanism" (Lincoln, research portal)
Dual-phase oscillation hypothesis: aesthetic delight = oscillation between Suspension of Disbelief (SD) and Introspective Detached Contemplation (IDC). Based on MEG + fMRI evidence that art appreciation has two temporal phases (milliseconds apart). Rasa = the oscillation itself.
**Use:** Chapter 4 + Chapter 5. The laugh oscillates between "I'm in the joke" (SD) and "I see the structure" (IDC). Both phases are present in every laugh. Comedy requires both: you have to be IN the joke to feel the violation AND OUTSIDE it to see the resolution.

### Ganser — "The Aesthetics of Dance" (2022, Brill) — rasa in performance
Rasasūtra: "Rasa arises from the union of determinants, consequents, and transitory states." Sthāyibhāva = durable emotion (one per rasa). Rasika = cultivated spectator who can savor rasa. Bharata: only "connoisseurs" experience full rasa.
**Use:** Chapter 4. The audience quality problem: not everyone can taste every rasa. The room's expertise = which rasas are accessible. A hostile room can't taste veera (heroic). A sober room can't taste shringara (erotic). This is the room's macroharmony.

## LAUGHTER & SOCIAL SYNCHRONIZATION

### In-sync-through-laughter: fNIRS hyperscanning (2026, Frontiers)
Laughter behavior associated with social connection and neural synchrony — but does NOT establish that laughter *causes* synchrony. Mechanism more complicated than "brains sync when we laugh."
**Use:** Chapter 8. Honest framing: laughter synchrony is real but the causal arrow is unclear. The comedian drives the oscillator; the laughter follows; whether laughter itself couples brains is unresolved.

### Neural and behavioral dynamics of dyadic rhythm coordination (limb-pairing study)
Interpersonal rhythmic coordination: paired limbs entrain through mutual prediction. Coordination = coupled oscillator dynamics. Same mechanism as musical entrainment.
**Use:** Chapter 8. The laugh delay harness (onset = beat end + 250ms base + comprehension jitter − contagion) is a coupled-oscillator model. The room and the comedian are coupled oscillators with a delay function.

### Is neural entrainment to rhythms the basis of social bonding?
Rhythmic synchrony → endogenous opioid release → social bonding. Music and laughter both use this system. Drumming together and laughing together activate the same bonding chemistry.
**Use:** Chapter 8 + Chapter 13 (conservation laws). Trust is conserved because it's chemically bonded through synchrony. Breaking synchrony (a joke that fails) costs trust — that's why failed jokes are expensive.

## JAZZ TIMING & COMEDY TIMING

### Pope, Stewart & Chew — "Timing Structures in Live Comedy" (2026, Oxford Academic — Music & Science)
Methods from musical performance analysis applied to stand-up. Performers combine stable repeated material with flexible sections. Audience interaction changes timing. A comedy set behaves like a musical score with room for improvisation.
**Use:** Chapter 9 (pause ladder). The direct evidence: comedy has measurable timing structure. Stable material + flexible sections = the same as a jazz head + solo. Audience interaction = the timing variable. This paper is the methodological bridge between the delivery engine and music analysis.

### 2009 acoustic study of humorous narratives (Tandfonline)
Punchlines delivered at lower pitch + slower speech rate than preceding material. NOT louder (as commonly assumed). The punch is a descent, not an explosion.
**Use:** Chapter 9. The punchline is a downward voice-leading move: lower pitch, slower rate, same volume. This is measurable and matches Tymoczko's minimal-displacement principle — the punch barely moves, it just moves *down*.

## PHILOSOPHY (the deep history)

### Kant — Critique of Judgment (1790)
Compares music and laughter as forms of pleasurable play involving rapidly changing representations and bodily effects. Their pleasures involve movement between expectations and transformations.
**Use:** Chapter 9 (philosophical endnote). Kant noticed the music-comedy connection 230 years ago. His explanation is outdated but the observation stands.

### Schopenhauer — The World as Will and Representation (1818)
Music bypasses the world of Ideas and expresses the Will directly. Tragedy reveals the nature of the world. Schopenhauer drew on Indian aesthetics (rasa theory) → influenced Nietzsche → influenced rasa reception in the West.
**Use:** Chapter 4 (cross-tradition bridge). The pipeline: Abhinavagupta → Schopenhauer → Nietzsche → Western aesthetics. Rasa theory arrived in the West through music philosophy.

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — "Humor" entry
Traces the full history: Plato, Aristotle, Kant, Schopenhauer, incongruity, superiority, relief theories. The philosophical foundation of humor studies.
**Use:** Chapter 9. The philosophical genealogy the book sits in.

## TYMOCZKO (the template — already in library/geometry/)

### Tymoczko — A Geometry of Music (2011, Oxford)
Chords as points in orbifold space. Voice leading as geodesics. Five components of tonality: conjunct motion, acoustic consonance, harmonic consistency, limited macroharmony, centricity. Near-symmetrical chords (augmented, diminished, whole-tone) = maximum voice-leading options. Modulation = voice-leading between scales. The book's central insight: music is not arbitrary — it lives in a structured geometric space, and compositional choices are movements through that space.
**Use:** The entire book's template. Every chapter maps a Tymoczko concept to comedy. The analysis at library/geometry/ANALYSIS-TYMOCZKO.md maps his five components to a 6D composition system — the same move we make for comedy.
