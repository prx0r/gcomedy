# The Geometry of Comedy — Outline

## Part I: The Space

### 1. Chords in Idea-Space
Tymoczko's move: chords are points in orbifold space, voice leading is a geodesic. Comedy's move: setups are points in idea-space, punchlines are geodesics to violation. The distance between "my therapist" and "is a printer" is measurable — and that's what makes it funny.

### 2. The Five Components of Laughter
Tymoczko's five, restated for comedy:

| Music component | Comedy component |
|---|---|
| Conjunct melodic motion | Minimal violation distance (the punch barely moves from the setup) |
| Acoustic consonance | Benign-violation fit (the violation "clicks" — it's bad but safe) |
| Harmonic consistency | Thematic consistency (the bit stays in one world) |
| Limited macroharmony | Limited target space (persona repertoire, comic range) |
| Centricity | Comic center (the persona's core — the field everything orbits) |

### 3. Orbifolds of the Absurd
Joke space wraps around itself. Absurdist comedy, surrealism, the "wait what" beat — these exploit the orbifold's identifications. Two distant ideas are the same point if you rotate the right way. Callback is a literal orbifold move: returning to the same point via a different path.

## Part II: The Tonalities

### 4. Nine Rasas, One Laugh
Indian aesthetic theory mapped: comedy is hasya (comic) but it never lives alone. Roast is hasya+raudra (comic+furious). Deadpan is hasya+shanta (comic+peaceful). The transgressive edge is hasya+bibhatsa (comic+odious). A comedian's range is their macroharmony — the set of rasas they can voice without modulating away from their center.

### 5. Camatkāra: The Wonder Equation
The Tantraloka says reality is aesthetic rapture (camatkāra) all the way down. The laugh is a micro-camatkāra — a moment where consciousness recognizes its own freedom in the structure of the violation. The setup constructs a prison. The punch is the key turning. That click of recognition — "of course" — is wonder compressed into a syllable.

### 6. The Target Field
Every joke has a target: self, powerful, species, or victim (the jokegate taxonomy). The geometry constrains which targets are playable from which positions. A comedian's stance in idea-space determines their accessible violations — this is the benign-violation manifold, and stepping off it is how sets die.

## Part III: The Time Axis

### 7. Rhythm and the Beat of a Bit
Setup → acceleration → tiny silence → punch → freeze → laugh → tag. This is the beat structure (from the delivery engine). The silence before the punch is the pick-up beat. The freeze after is the resolution. Laughter is the downbeat the room lands on together.

### 8. Neural Entrainment: When the Room Breathes as One
fNIRS hyperscanning proves laugh synchrony is real neural coupling, not just social mimicry. Dyadic rhythm coordination (limb-pairing studies) shows the same entrainment mechanism. The comedian isn't telling jokes to a room — they're driving an oscillator network. The laugh delay harness (onset = beat end + 250ms base + comprehension jitter − contagion) is the entrainment model in code.

### 9. The Pause Ladder
Measured delivery geometry: pause_after_ms per beat type, punchline holds (450–1100ms), the ramp. The same joke with different pauses is a different voicing — like a chord played legato vs staccato. This is why the same material can kill or die depending on timing, and why delivery is a measurable geometry, not a vibe.

## Part IV: The Players

### 10. Character as Timbre
A character graph (persona + misinterpretation rule + operator priors + voice) is a timbre. The same punch voiced by Ralph (reptilian middle-management: "files cruelty as HR") vs Diogenes (tub cynic: "your virtue is a haircut") is the same chord in a different voice. Persona is the harmonic series that colors every note.

### 11. Callback as Harmonic Return
The callback economy: every ending, walk-in, object enters the archive. Later performance invokes it — same idea, new voicing. First invocation pays ×1.5. Repeat pays zero. This is voice-leading economy: the move is short because the idea already exists in the space. The room's recognition ("I caught that") is the consonance of return.

### 12. Improv Geometry: Freeze Night as Chord Progression
Freeze Night is a progression: scene → freeze → prompt → winner unfreezes → scene continues. Each winning prompt is a chord change. The night's archive is the accumulated macroharmony. The champion has navigated the most consonant path through idea-space. The wheel of shame is the tritone — the punishment that makes the resolution land.

## Part V: The Laws

### 13. Conservation Laws
What's conserved across a set: energy (the room's heat), attention (one task at a time), trust (the benign-violation contract). Break any conservation law and the set dies. The guitar-hero rule (room energy with resistance, melts, hysteresis) is the thermodynamics of comedy.

### 14. Composing: From Geometry to Bits
How to write using the geometry: measure the violation distance, check the rasa, time the pause, voice it for the persona, plant the callback. The book ends where Tymoczko ends — not with analysis but with composition. The geometry isn't a cage. It's the score.
