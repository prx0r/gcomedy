# EPISODE 2 — THE ART OF THE SETUP (14 min, locked)

> Thesis: **A setup is a shared world being built in real time — and the
> audience builds half of it themselves without knowing.**
> Vocabulary reservoir: premise, platform, weight, priority, shared world,
> AAB (two beats install, third breaks), setup, punch. (8 terms. Nothing
> else new after halfway.)
> Runtime: 14 min (840s board). Narration ~1,400 words + clips/pauses.
