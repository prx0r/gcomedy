## THEORY (accessible gloss — names stay in overlays + notes, never narration)

- **Iser gap / co-creation (reader-response).** Texts leave blanks — unwritten portions between perspectives. Readers fill them, and the filling IS the work: expectations get frustrated, the reader connects the segments, then re-reads the past in the new light. Narration gloss (no names): "the room builds half of it without knowing" (established series line, setup ESSAY) → "the joke leaves a blank and the room fills it — the laugh is the sound of the filling." Sources: Iser "The Reading Process" / The Act of Reading (blanks/gaps, wandering viewpoint, implied reader); accessible via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Iser (fetched 2026-10-18). Overlay label only: `gap`.
- **Sphoṭa (Bhartṛhari) — the re-weighting flash (paper unnamed in narration).** The sentence is grasped whole, in a flash, and the whole re-weights what came before — meaning arrives backward. Narration gloss (no names, no Sanskrit in voiceover): "the punch doesn't give you new information — it gives you an entirely new past" (established series line, punch ESSAY). Technical gloss for notes/overlays: vakya-sphoṭa (sentence grasped whole) + pratibhā (flash of recognition). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spho%E1%B9%ADa (fetched 2026-10-18); Brhat "Sphoṭa & Pratibhā" gloss. Overlay label only: `re-weight`. The named paper is never spoken.
- **Inverted possibility space (framing).** The joke region sits OUTSIDE the real — what's impossible defines what's funny. The diagram (possibility map, real region marked, joke region outside) is a new instrument — VISUAL-GRAMMAR amendment required before render (STRUCTURE.md data honesty). Narration uses only plain words: "where reality can't go." Source pointer: library/comedy/INVERTED-POSSIBILITY-SPACE.md (material-first/undeliverable-bit framing in repo; episode uses its payoff gloss — the undeliverable region — not its pipeline).
- **What narration may say.** Bleed (found, not made), noticing (daily capture), gap (room fills the blank), re-weight (past reorganizes). Four craft claims, plain words, no authors.
