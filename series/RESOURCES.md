## PAPERS (per function)

### Setup / Premise
- **Pollio & Mers 1974** — predictability correlates POSITIVELY with laughter (Cosby/Diller in-concert). "Predictable unpredictability." *Independent replication of our Exp 3 (r=+0.33).* Cite in Setup video.
- **Kenny 1955** (via Pollio) — predictable jokes rated funniest. Same direction, older.

### Punch / Timing
- **Attardo & Pickering 2011** — timing = pauses + speech rate (20 jokes). Punchlines NOT different rate; pre-punch emphatic pause REJECTED in their sample. *Nuance for Pause video: their lab jokes differ from our set-scale data (14x holds) — scale matters, say so.*
- **Zhong 2026 (Speech Prosody)** — Mandarin stand-up: pre-punch pauses +157ms in production, NO perceptual advantage in listening tests. Production–perception mismatch. *Use: timing is for the room, not the recording.*
- **Marinkovic 2011 (aMEG)** — funny punchlines: SMALLER N400 (primed surface congruity) then right-prefrontal reprocessing (distant coherence). *Neural confirmation of minimal-distance + reinterpretation: the setup primes, the punch re-routes right.*
- **Sawahata et al. 2013 (PLOS ONE)** — humor decodable from fMRI 2s BEFORE awareness (right DLPFC + temporal). *The expectation state precedes the laugh — the tension bar is neurally real.*

### Escalation / Tags
- **McKeague 2021 (Comic Triple framework)** — setup/setup/contradiction as linear process; rule-of-three formalized. *Feeds Escalation video; note our correction (4+ = different machine).*
- **Alnajjar et al. 2022 (COLING)** — multimodal humor detection 78%, laughter-intensity MAE 600ms (Friends laugh-track corpus). *Computational baseline; intensity-prediction is learnable.*

### Callbacks
- Yagoda (Nieman) on callback craft — Mulaney dogs/horses, Vonnegut mustard-gas-and-roses (already in essay). No new paper needed; craft sources suffice.
