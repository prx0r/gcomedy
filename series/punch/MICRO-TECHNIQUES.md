# PUNCH MICRO-TECHNIQUES — good vs bad, banked

> Sources: Marinkovic N400 (primed surface congruity → right-prefrontal
> reprocessing), Exp 2 (r=−0.32 punch length), Twain (slur the nub),
> Hedberg/Wright one-liner economy, Jeselnik third thought.

## GOOD PUNCH vs BAD (the video's spine)

| Good | Bad |
|---|---|
| Shortest path to a disjoint reading | Explained punch (second sentence clarifying the first) |
| Lands on the final word (funnest word last) | Punch word buried mid-sentence |
| One relabeling, whole world changes | Two relabelings (room tracks one; second is noise) |
| Surprising yet inevitable in retrospect | Random (no path back) or telegraphed (no travel) |
| Commits (lands and stops) | Apologizes (hedge, grin, "you know?") |

## Micro-technique bank

1. **Funnest-word-last.** Reorder so the relabeling word ends the sentence.
   Demo: same punch, two word orders, room meters differ. Non-negotiable.
2. **Slur the nub (Twain/Artemus Ward).** Drop the punch casually, pretend
   you don't know it's the nub. Let the room catch up — the half-second
   delay IS the laugh forming.
3. **Third thought (Jeselnik).** First thought obvious, second worked-for,
   third one-step-removed (never-would-have-thought). 3 jokes/day mornings.
4. **Surface congruity first (Marinkovic).** Funny punches show SMALLER
   N400 — prime the surface reading hard so the twist has further to fall.
   A punch the setup didn't earn reads as non sequitur, not surprise.
5. **One assumption shifted (broken-assumptions audit).** Name which
   who/what/where/when/why/how flips. Exactly one. Two flips = confusion.
6. **Punch-length discipline (r=−0.32).** Every word past the minimum costs
   score. Cut to the relabeling word + landing pad, nothing else.
7. **No-telegraph check.** If the room can finish your sentence, rewrite
   the setup (hide the path) or change the punch (new destination).
8. **Commit-and-stop.** Land, silence, do not touch it. Explaining,
   grinning, or rushing on reads as retraction — the room un-laughs.
9. **Pun superposition (center of joke-space).** Keep both readings live
   instead of selecting — ambiguity + distinctiveness both high. Rare,
   highest density per word in comedy.
10. **Autopsy drill.** Keep setup, write five alternate final lines. Four
    will be worse. The exercise teaches minimal travel better than theory.
