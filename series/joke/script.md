## [1:30] THESIS + VOCABULARY

Eight words for this series, then no new jargon ever: one-liner, joke, bit, chunk, set, setup, punch, tag. A one-liner is setup plus punch in a single sentence. A joke is the atom — one of each, one laugh point. A bit is premise plus several jokes plus tags, minutes long. A chunk is bits on one topic — ten, fifteen minutes. A set is the night. Setup and punch you already know — blue and red. Tag is the echo after the break, and it gets its own video.

> DIR: OVERLAY glossary cards fan out; persist in description.
