# CALLBACKS MICRO-TECHNIQUES — good vs bad, banked

> Sources: Yagoda (Nieman), Mulaney dogs/horses,
> Acaster tsunami, Lee failed-callback, Bergson memory distinction
> (production notes only).

## GOOD CALLBACK vs BAD (the video's spine)

| Good | Bad |
|---|---|
| Returns changed (revoiced by everything since) | Microwaved rehash (same words, same context) |
| 2–8 min gap heuristic (retrieval + surprise both live — corpus query pending) | Too early (echo, no retrieval) or too late (homework) |
| Signposted (turn of phrase/gesture says "we're back") | Esoteric (half the room missed the original) |
| Fits the new context (makes local sense AND recalls) | Parasitic (only funny if you remember; dead otherwise) |
| Rewards attention (room feels smart) | Punishes absence (latecomers confused, punished) |

## Micro-technique bank

1. **Revoice or die.** Same material, new meaning. Change context, angle,
   or polarity. Test: cover the original — does the line still function?
   If yes alone, it's not a callback, it's a repeat.
2. **The clock (2–8 min).** Under 90s reads as running gag (no retrieval).
   Past 15 min needs half a setup of reminding. Big moments survive longer;
   small moments need shorter gaps. Re-plant halfway to reset the clock.
3. **Signpost the return.** Identifiable turn of phrase, gesture, or
   musical sting. The room must know where it's being taken within 2
   seconds or retrieval never starts.
4. **Bury batteries (headliner practice).** Plant big laugh lines early
   even unneeded — they're stored power for sagging middles. Closer runs
   on stored power, not new material.
5. **Wide before deep.** Tsunami finales: twelve threads one level each
   beats one thread three levels. Stacks past depth-2 collapse (retrieval
   cost exceeds payoff). Breadth scales; depth doesn't.
6. **Failed-callback gambit (Lee).** Bomb one deliberately; the wreckage
   smuggles 20 minutes of revelation. Advanced: requires total control —
   the failure must read accidental while being engineered.
7. **Rescue call.** Mid-set sag → drop an early big laugh's callback.
   Present + retrieved amusement stack (two laughs, one reference).
8. **Mulaney shape (dogs/horses).** Plant absurd, wait 40 minutes, return
   minimal ("dogs without horses" — 3 words carrying 40). Study the ratio:
   maximum distance, minimum words.
9. **Mulaney shape (plant absurd, return minimal).** Absurd original, bare
   return — gap measured at build; revoicing by subtraction.
10. **Close the loop or cut it.** Every planted callback either pays by
    the finale or gets cut in rewrite. Unpaid plants are broken promises;
    rooms remember being owed.
