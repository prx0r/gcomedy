# STATUS MICRO-TECHNIQUES — good vs bad, banked

## GOOD STATUS vs BAD
| Good | Bad |
|---|---|
| Status clear in 30 seconds (room knows the hierarchy) | Status muddy (who's up, who's down — unknowable) |
| Punch up (shared enemy, justice+timing) | Punch down (bullying reads, room sides with target) |
| Status shifts earned (event causes the fall/rise) | Arbitrary shifts (hierarchy reshuffled for convenience) |
| Self as lowest when appropriate (confession anchor) | Self as shield (self-deprecation deflecting real targets) |
| High-status characters played low (gap = comedy) | Low-status characters played high with no frame (confusion) |

## Bank
1. **Declare hierarchy fast.** First 30 seconds: who is above whom (you/boss/crowd/world). No declaration = no violations possible.
2. **Punch up, never down (comedywriter.info).** Shared enemy unites; victim isolates. Sideways only when drunk (theirs).
3. **Status_invert cleanly.** Falls need events; rises need permission. Arbitrary shifts read as writing, not happening.
4. **Self-low anchor.** Periodic self-lowering buys license for outward hits. Ratio ~1:3 (self:other).
5. **Gap comedy.** High character, low situation (or reverse). The distance between status claimed and status shown IS the bit.
6. **Room-above-you sets.** Corporate/clean crowds outrank the comic: submit playfully (Regan technician), never challenge.
7. **Room-below-you sets.** Drunk Friday: shepherd, don't lecture. Authority without contempt.
8. **Character POV status (Meltzer).** Each POV carries its own rank. Shifts = re-ranks; announce with body, not words.
9. **Locke oscillation.** Protected (grumpy baseline) ↔ unprotected (elastic chaos). The swing, not either pole, is the engine.
10. **Status audit.** List every target's rank vs yours. Any down-punch either re-aims up or cuts. No exceptions tabled.
