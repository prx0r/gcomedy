# CONFESSION MICRO-TECHNIQUES — good vs bad, banked

## GOOD CONFESSION vs BAD
| Good | Bad |
|---|---|
| True humiliation, structured as jokes (laugh points intact) | Unstructured pain-sharing (captive-audience therapy) |
| Vulnerability with craft (every wound has a punchline) | Vulnerability as shield (honesty deflecting weak writing) |
| Packaging matches box (voice honestly represents self) | Packaging lies (persona contradicts confessions) |
| Punchlines per wound (no wound unbilled) | Wound monologues (pain with no laugh points) |
| Earned (room trusts you first, then you open) | Premature (confession before connection = overshare) |

## Bank
1. **O'Neal authenticity.** True + righteous > true alone. Have a point of view about the wound, not just the wound.
2. **Packaging honesty (Comedyfight).** Voice must represent self accurately. Contradictions kill trust; trust is the precondition for confession landing.
3. **Punchline-per-wound rule.** Every revealed wound ships with ≥1 laugh point. Unbilled pain gets cut or billed (rewrite until funny).
4. **Earn it first.** 5+ minutes of competence/laughs before first real confession. Trust precedes vulnerability, always.
5. **Personal POV switch (Cross).** Clever→personal transition is a career move: wit opens rooms, confession keeps them. Time it deliberately.
6. **Overshare gate.** Would this work for a stranger with no sympathy for you? If it needs their pity, it's therapy. Cut or joke it.
7. **Exaggerate the reaction, not the wound (workshop rule).** Truth stays; your response to it gets the cartoon. Wound verbatim + reaction 10x.
8. **Confessional +0.2 (jokegate).** Earnest self-target buys distance. Performative self-target spends it. Earnestness is detectable; don't fake the frame.
9. **One open wound per set.** Multiple simultaneous confessions diffuse. Pick the wound; subordinate the scars to tags.
10. **Close the wound on stage.** Confession arc needs a button (acceptance/reframe/joke). Open wounds walked off with read as cries for help.
