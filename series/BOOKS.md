# BOOK SHELF — why these functions are pillars

> Searched 2026-10-18. These are the books that establish each function
> as load-bearing. Read the starred ones first.

## Why the six functions stand (one line each)
- **Setup/premise:** Vorhaus (comic premise) + O'Shannon (gap-filling,
  recontextualization) + Rock's rule. Without shared world, no operations.
- **Escalation:** Libera (Second City heightening) + improv game theory.
  Repetition with rising stakes is the engine of long-form.
- **Punch:** Suls (incongruity-resolution) + Martin (setup/punch structure)
  + Twain (slurring the nub). Minimal travel, maximal rearrangement.
- **Tags:** Dean (tag/edit pipeline) + Kalan (rewrite counts). Discounted
  laughs off built platforms.
- **Callbacks:** O'Shannon (entropy/sustaining the laugh) + memory theory.
  Recognition pleasure, revoiced returns.
- **Button:** Libera (running order, hierarchy of needs) + cadence theory.
  Closure discharges open trajectories.

## The shelf
1. ★ O'Shannon, *What Are You Laughing At?* (Continuum) — unifies
   incongruity/superiority/relief; gap-filling + recontextualization +
   entropy. The theory spine.
2. ★ Libera, *Funnier* (Northwestern) — Second City method: recognition,
   pain, distance; thick vs thin; premise muscles; running order.
3. ★ McGraw & Warner, *The Humor Code* — benign violation, global field
   test of the theory.
4. Martin, *Psychology of Humor* — the textbook; setup/punch canon.
5. Palmer, *Taking Humour Seriously* — culture-level grounding.
6. Twain, *How to Tell a Story* (1895, PD) — humorous vs comic story;
   the pause "exactly the right length."
7. Vorhaus, *The Comic Toolbox* — premise, truth+pain, rule of nine.
8. Kalan, *Joke Farming* — structure/premise/voice/tone/wording/audience.
9. Dean, *Step by Step to Stand-Up* — 5 mechanisms, tag pipeline.
10. Forbes 21-books list (Aswell 2019) — Holloway, Carter + 18 more.
